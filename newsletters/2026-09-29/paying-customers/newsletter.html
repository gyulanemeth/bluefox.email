Should you switch?
Yes, if you buy Essential packs and send steadily. Every plan from Basic up costs less per send.
Yes, if you buy Premium packs and send 100,000 or more a month. Business, Scale and Elite cost less per send. At 500,000 a month, Elite saves you $732 a year.
No, if your volume comes in bursts. Plan allowances reset every billing cycle and unused sends do not roll over. Packs stay valid for 12 months, so they suit uneven sending better.
