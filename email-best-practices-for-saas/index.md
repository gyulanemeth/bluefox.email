# Email Best Practices for SaaS

If you're working in SaaS, you know that email is a crucial tool for staying connected with your customers. Whether you're sending a transaction confirmation, a behavior-triggered reminder, or launching a marketing campaign, each email contributes to the overall customer experience and to your sender reputation. These guides walk through the patterns that keep your audience engaged and protect your deliverability.

## Guides

- **[Double Opt-In](./double-opt-in)** — Why confirming new subscribers protects your list quality and deliverability, and how to put it in place.
- **[Unsubscribe and Pause Subscription](./unsubscribe-and-pause-subscription)** — Making it easy to leave, and offering a pause instead of a full unsubscribe, lowers spam complaints and keeps your list healthy.
- **[DMARC for SaaS](./dmarc)** — Rolling out DMARC as a SaaS team that sends through third-party providers, from a monitoring-only policy to full enforcement.
